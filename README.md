## 🚀 Overview

> **Stop managing tasks. Start conquering quests.**
> 
> NOVA LIST transforms boring, everyday productivity into an RPG-inspired experience. Every completed task earns you gold, unlocks progression, and pushes you closer to becoming a legendary warrior.

---

## 🛡️ Core Features

* **⚔️ Quest System:** Create custom quests, set difficulties, and track your daily missions with ease.
* **🪙 Gold Economy:** Earn tangible rewards and gold for every successfully completed mission.
* **🏆 Level System:** Unlock higher ranks and show off your dedication as you gain experience.
* **⏳ Focus Timer:** Built-in 25-minute Pomodoro-style focus mode to keep you in the zone.
* **💾 Local Storage:** Your progress is safe. Automatically saves all data locally so you never lose your stats.
* **📱 Responsive UI:** A beautiful, modern, and adaptive dashboard that works seamlessly across all devices.

## 🧠 Why NOVA LIST Exists

Most To-Do apps feel like digital paperwork. Tasks are added, tasks are checked, but nothing feels rewarding. 

NOVA LIST was built around a simple philosophy:
> **"Productivity should feel like progression."**
> 
> Every action gives instant feedback, every achievement feels earned, and every session moves the player forward in their real-life RPG.
